# Silence, Please — The White Hart

A playable PixiJS prototype of a very quiet London pub with a rather noisy Silencer.

**Play now:** [sergutsu.github.io/Silence-Please](https://sergutsu.github.io/Silence-Please/)

## Open the pub

There is no install, bundler, or build step. Open `index.html` directly, or serve this folder with any static web server. PixiJS and the game are included in the repository; the game does not fetch a remote runtime or game assets.

## Wednesday service

- Tap a patron in the illustrated room, pull their pint at the Beer Tap, then tap that same patron again.
- Tap any open room to turn its Silencer Zone on or off. Silenced rooms read 0 dB; their absorbed noise fills the Silence Energy meter.
- At 70%, 85%, and 95% capacity the warnings get progressively less polite. At 100%, the Silencer explodes.
- Spend Silence Energy on filters, microphones, a vent, reinforced casing, or the one-use Emergency Dump. Turn on the Vent when a safe vent rate is available.
- Open the Landlord’s Desk to buy rooms, Silencer research, staff, and amenities. Swipe or use the arrows beneath the pub to see other rooms.
- Pause with the header button or the space bar. Long-press a patron for their patience, noise, and trade. Progress is saved in this browser.

The project vendors the PixiJS 8.6.6 browser distribution under the MIT License at `vendor/PIXIJSLICENSE.txt`. The game itself is plain browser JavaScript and CSS.
